## Why this request

The public data sets can say how many people major in math at each college, what it costs, and
what graduates earn. They cannot say what a math class is like. A high school junior would want
to know how big the math classes are, especially the first-year ones. A college keeps that record
(its course enrollment report), and a public college's records can be requested under the Open
Public Records Act (N.J.S.A. 47:1A-1).

The request goes to one college, The College of New Jersey. The other 12 colleges would each need
their own request, so there is no benchmark for this number. The dashboard would say that.

## The request (draft wording)

> Under the Open Public Records Act, I request electronic copies of the following record, in
> Excel or CSV format if it is kept that way: the course section enrollment report for all
> undergraduate course sections with the subject code MAT or STA at The College of New Jersey
> for the Fall 2025 and Spring 2026 semesters, showing for each section the course number,
> course title, section number, enrollment capacity, and number of students enrolled.
>
> I am not requesting the names of students or instructors. If the report includes instructor
> names, please remove that column.
>
> This is a class project, not a commercial request. Please let me know of any charge before
> filling this request.

Notes on the wording:

- It asks for a record (an enrollment report), not an answer ("how big are math classes?").
- It names the kind of record, the subject codes, and two semesters.
- It asks for a spreadsheet and to be told about any charge first.
- It asks for no names. If names come back anyway, delete that column before the file goes
  anywhere near the repository.
- The requester's own name and contact information go on the form. Anonymous requesters cannot
  appeal a denial.


## What the dashboard would do with the records

- One row per course section. Check the key (course number + section + semester).
- A new figure for TCNJ only: the number of students in each MAT and STA section, with the

- No benchmark, because the other colleges were not asked. The text under the figure would say so.
- If the request is denied or only partly filled, the dashboard would say what was asked for and
